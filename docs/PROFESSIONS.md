# Secondary profession guides

Cooking, Fishing, First Aid and Archaeology guides for all five clients, generated by `tools/wiki/gen_professions.py` into
`Guides/Imported_Professions_<flavor>.lua` (gitignored, CC BY-SA 4.0, listed in `Guides/WIKI_DATA_LICENSE.md`).

| Client | Professions | Bands |
|---|---|---|
| era, forever | Cooking, Fishing, First Aid | Apprentice to Artisan, 1-300 |
| tbc | Cooking, Fishing, First Aid | adds Master, to 375 |
| mop | Cooking, Fishing, First Aid, Archaeology | Apprentice to Zen Master, to 600 |
| retail | Cooking, Fishing (one set per expansion tier), Archaeology | tier caps from the wiki; Archaeology to 950 |

First Aid does not exist on retail (wiki: removed in patch 8.0.1).

## Regenerate

```
/Users/daniel/.claude/jobs/<job>/tmp/heavy.sh python3 tools/wiki/gen_professions.py [era tbc mop retail forever]
luajit tools/test_professions.lua
```

Any `heavy.sh`-style lock will do on a small machine; the retail tables are about 65 MB of CSV. Wiki pages and client tables are
cached (`tools/wiki/cache/extra_pages`, `tools/db2/<flavor>`), so a second run makes no requests. Each run replaces that flavor's
rows in the `prof_guides` table of `docs/verification.sqlite`.

## Trainer rules

A trainer is kept on a client only when all of these hold:

* its landmass and row badges are no newer than the client;
* its row is not restricted (starting zones only, event only, one specialty recipe, a Pandaria "Way of" master);
* its NPC page does not say it was added after this client's game version, or that it no longer trains;
* on era, tbc and forever, a page saying it moved at the Cataclysm is dropped unless the wiki also gives a pre-Cataclysm coordinate;
* its coordinate lands on a map this client has. Retail coordinates are carried across through world coordinates (`wikimaps.Atlas`).

When the wiki has both a Classic coordinate and a retail one more than 300 yards apart, the Classic one wins. A rank limit in the
notes column (the Train-a-Tron stops at Journeyman) removes that trainer from higher ranks. The counts of dropped trainers are printed per flavor.

## Plan algorithm

For one band the planner walks the skill one point at a time and picks a recipe that can still give skill: learned at or below the
current skill and below its grey threshold.

* The estimated chance of a skill point is 1 below yellow, then `(grey - skill) / (grey - yellow)`. That is the community
  approximation; the wiki itself only says orange is certain, yellow "often", green "seldom" and grey never.
* A recipe's planning weight is its reagent count, with vendor goods cheapest, a named source next, and an unknown source most
  expensive, then multiplied by how it is learned (automatic and trainer cheapest). Weights are internal and are never shown as prices.
* The cheapest weight per expected skill point wins. The current recipe is kept while it is within 35% of the best, so a guide does not change dish every point.
* Recipes with no source on the wiki, seasonal recipes and recipes limited to the other faction are left out and counted.
* A recipe's learn skill is the higher of the wiki value and the client's `MinSkillLineRank`. With no wiki value the yellow threshold is
  used, because a recipe is always learned at or below its yellow. That can only delay a recipe, never use it too early.
* On era, tbc and forever, a recipe the wiki calls trainer-taught today becomes a buy step when this client has a recipe item for it.
  The wiki describes retail, and most of those recipes moved to trainers in later patches.
* The wiki writes expansion recipes on the retail per-expansion scale, so on the one-track clients a learn value more than 120 below the client's yellow is discarded and yellow is used.
* A band the recipes cannot reach is cut short: `skillMax` and the guide name show where it really stops, and the gap is counted.

## Step grammar actually emitted

`docs/GUIDE_FORMAT.md` is the contract. One deliberate difference from the first draft of the grammar:

> **Make steps carry `|SPELL|id|`, not `|RECIPE|id|`.** The engine completes a step when either `SKILL` or `RECIPE` holds, and a
> character making a dish already knows it, so `M Dish|SKILL|...|RECIPE|...|` would tick the moment it became current.
> `tools/test_professions.lua` fails if a make step carries both, and fails if the engine's either-or behaviour ever changes, so this can be revisited then.

`RECIPE` is used on train and buy steps, where "already known" really does mean done.

## Archaeology at runtime

`Core/Archaeology.lua` registers one guide, "Archaeology: active dig sites".

* It reads `C_ResearchInfo.GetDigSitesForMap` for each zone of the player's continent, and lists the sites nearest-first.
* If the client has no such API or reports nothing, it falls back to the generated `NS.ArchaeologySites` list, capped at the 12 nearest, and says so in the note.
* It rebuilds on `RESEARCH_ARTIFACT_DIG_SITE_UPDATED`, `ARTIFACT_DIG_SITE_UPDATED`, `ARTIFACT_DIGSITE_COMPLETE` and a continent change.
* Sites are `M` steps, not proximity waypoints: arriving must not tick a site that still has finds in it.
* The older landmark API (`GetNumMapLandmarks`, `GetMapLandmarkInfo`) is not used. The wiki lists it as removed in 8.0.1, and it reads the map chosen with `SetMapByID`, which takes old world-map area ids this addon does not hold. Whether MoP Classic exposes `C_ResearchInfo` could not be confirmed offline; where it does not, the guide uses the static list.

## Limits

* **Nothing here has run in a real client.** `tools/test_professions.lua` runs the real addon code against stubs.
* Whether a recipe is trainer-taught comes from the retail-first wiki. The recipe-item check corrects most Classic cases, but a recipe that was neither on a trainer nor an item there would still say Train.
* Many retail recipes have no usable source on the wiki and are left out, so several retail Cooking bands stop short of the tier cap. See `prof_guides.skill_max` and `missing_facts`.
* Named NPC or quest teachers (for example Sylann) are shown as a note with no arrow.
* On era and tbc some small-town trainers stop at a low rank. The wiki table no longer says which, so ALT lists them all and the step says to try a city trainer.
* Skill-up chances and craft counts are estimates. Reagent amounts follow from them.
* `Fishing locations` has no rows after Cataclysm. Retail tiers from Pandaria on point at the expansion's continent map, not a specific lake.
* `Cooking trainers` is marked out of date on the wiki and lists no trainers for most tiers after Pandaria. Those guides say so and carry no ALT.
* "Way of" Pandaria recipes sit in their own skill lines and are not planned; mop Zen Master Cooking therefore leans on the expensive banquet recipes.
* Forever's own higher rank spells (1240945 and siblings) are not used; Forever guides stop at 300 like era.
