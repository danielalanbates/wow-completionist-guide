# Quest counts, the order of the routes list, and prefill

Built on 2026-11-13 (branch `quest-counts`). Offline-tested only: nothing here has run in a WoW client.

Code: `Core/QuestCounts.lua` (what is counted), `UI/GuideMenu.lua` (the list), `Core/Progress.lua` `P.Prefill`,
`Core/Account.lua` (`A.QuestsChanged`, `A.GuideIsComplete`), `tools/gen_quest_index.lua` (the generated index),
`tools/test_quest_counts.lua` (the tests).

## 1. What the routes list shows

| Row | Right-hand text |
|---|---|
| Top of the window, under the scope buttons | `Quests done: 312 of 4,268`: every quest of every route this character can see, each quest id once |
| Folder (Leveling, Quests, a zone ...) | `312 / 1,040 quests`: the quests of the routes under it, each quest id once. A folder with no quest routes (Gold, Professions) shows `3 routes done` or nothing. It never says "0 completed" |
| Quest route | `12 / 40 quests`, or `completed` when all of them are done |
| Gold circuit, profession route, achievement list, mission table | as before (`8 stops`, a percentage, `completed`) |

`...` means "not counted yet". The Character / Server / Game type / Account buttons decide whose completed quests
count; the numbers are counted again when the scope changes.

## 2. What counts as a quest

* **A route's quests** are the quest ids its steps name in a `QID` tag, each id once. Accept, objective and turn-in
  steps all count, so a quest the route only accepts is still one of its quests.
* **Left out:** steps marked optional (`|O|`); steps for another faction, class or race (`FACTION`, `C`, `R` tags), so
  a both-factions route shows an Alliance character only the quests an Alliance character can take; quests this
  character dropped with `/cr skip` (a quest that a drop only strands inside one route is still counted).
* **Only quest routes are counted.** A quest route turns at least one quest in and is not a gold or profession
  route. Achievement lists name quests as criteria, and many of those ids are hidden tracking flags; a profession
  route may hold one trainer quest. Neither adds to a folder or to the total.
* **Done** means completed by a character in scope: this character's completed quests (read from the client by
  `Account.HarvestCompleted`, plus every `QUEST_TURNED_IN`), and in the wider scopes the saved lists of the other
  characters the scope covers. The scope alone decides this; the option "also match by quest ID across different
  routes" only affects which steps are ticked.
* **A quest in several routes** counts in each of those routes, and once in a folder and in the total. A leveling
  chain entry and the zone route it is made from share every quest, so the Leveling folder and the Quests folder each
  count them, and the total counts them once.
* **Hidden and tracking quest ids.** The client's completed list holds thousands of ids no route names. They are
  never counted: the total is "quests of the routes", not "ids the client has flagged".
* **A quest route is finished** when every one of its quests is done by the characters in scope. Before this change
  it was finished when one character had turned in every quest it turns in; the difference is a quest the route
  accepts but has no turn-in step for (about 800 on Retail), and two characters who each did half.

## 3. Where the numbers come from, and what they cost

`Data/QuestIndex_<flavor>.lua` is generated by `tools/gen_quest_index.lua`. It has one short line per route:

```
["wiki:era:1416:Alliance"]="2446||1:510,512,522,504,523"   -- text length | ids for everyone (none here) | restriction 1: ids
["gold:era:fish:1439:19"]="519"                             -- a route that turns no quest in: text length only
```

`conds[n]` spells restriction `n` as `faction<TAB>class list<TAB>race list`. A leveling chain entry has no line of
its own: it reads its zone route's.

* No quest route is parsed for its count. The first line is decoded once per route and kept.
* A route that turns no quest in is finished by its ticks alone, so one nobody in scope has a tick in is known to be
  unfinished without parsing it. Before, the list parsed every route once per session to learn that.
* The length in front is the staleness check. If the route files are regenerated and the index is not, the lengths
  differ, the entries are ignored and the list falls back to parsing those routes (`QC.Groups`, the same function the
  generator uses): slower, same numbers. Routes the index does not hold (live and hand-made routes, gold or
  profession routes that turn a quest in) are parsed as before.
* Everything runs in the background while the list is open, in slices of at most 4 ms a frame
  (`SCAN_MS`): first every route's count and "finished" answer, then one tally per folder row on screen and one for
  the total. Rows read stored answers only.

Measured offline under `luajit -joff` (the interpreter; the client's Lua 5.1 is slower per operation, the 4 ms slice
limit is the same), a character with a third of the quests done:

| Client | Routes seen | Quest routes | Quests counted | Scan with the index | Scan without it (as before) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| era (Alliance) | 344 | 193 | 1,945 | 2 frames, 4 ms | 276 ms, longest frame 11 ms |
| tbc (Alliance) | 450 | 263 | 3,122 | 3 frames, 8 ms | 449 ms, longest frame 11 ms |
| forever (Alliance) | 355 | 199 | 2,019 | 2 frames, 4 ms | 199 ms, longest frame 12 ms |
| mop (Alliance) | 809 | 470 | 7,022 | 5 frames, 15 ms | 797 ms, longest frame 24 ms |
| retail (Alliance) | 3,506 | 2,971 | 21,648 | 17 frames, 67 ms, longest 7 ms | 4,132 ms, longest frame 149 ms |

The folder tallies and the total took 1 to 7 more frames, none over 4.1 ms. "Quests counted" is what one Alliance
character can take; the index holds 3,302 (era), 5,024 (tbc), 3,489 (forever), 10,562 (mop) and 28,710 (retail)
quest ids for both factions.

Index sizes: era 30 KB, tbc 44 KB, forever 33 KB, mop 94 KB, retail 403 KB.

## 4. The order of the list

Inside every folder, and in the flat search results (`GuideMenu.RouteLess`):

1. Routes of the leveling chain first, in chain order (the number the route id ends in).
2. Everything else by the name the row shows, with numbers compared as numbers. A zone's parts are next to each
   other and run Part 1, 2, 3 ... 10, 11, 12. `Cooking 1-75` comes before `Cooking 75-150`. Two series of parts with
   the same shown name are not interleaved: the both-factions series first, then the faction series; two zones with
   one name (the two Silvermoon Cities on Retail) each keep their own run.
3. Gold routes sort by their full name, which still holds the level range the row does not show
   (`Mob Farming 10-15` before `Mob Farming 20-25`).
4. Ties: lowest level, then the full name, then the id.

Folders are sorted by name the same way (numbers as numbers) under the fixed category order.

## 5. Prefill

`P.Refresh` auto-completes a window of the next 40 open steps. That is enough while a route is played, and not
enough for a route picked up part-way. Found and fixed:

| Where it did not hold | Now |
|---|---|
| Steps of completed quests further than 40 open steps ahead stayed open until the window reached them | `P.Prefill` walks the whole list once and ticks every step whose quest is completed |
| A completed quest's step hidden by a level gate or an unmet `PRE` was never ticked, so the route was parked ("N more steps at level 58") or never read as finished | ticked like any other; applicability is not asked |
| A route loaded before the client's completed list arrived (first login on a Classic client) caught up only inside the window | every arrival of new completed quests (`A.questGen`) runs the prefill again |
| A quest turned in out of order left its other steps open far ahead | same |
| Steps a brought-back quest (`/cr unskip`) returns, when the quest was done meanwhile | the rebuild runs the prefill |
| Start over on a long route re-ticked only the window | the reset runs the prefill |

What it ticks: a step with a quest id whose quest is completed, whatever its action (accept, objective, kill,
turn-in, note); `1&2` needs both quests, `1^2` either. On a load it also ticks profession steps whose skill or
recipe is known and achievement steps that are earned. It does not tick travel, bag or position steps (those are
about now), a step the player stepped back to with the Back arrow, or anything in a farm circuit. Prefill is
catching up, not progress: it does not move a route up in Recent.

A route that was never opened shows the same thing in its row, from the counts above. The row does not say which
step the route would start at: that needs the route parsed, which the list avoids.

## 6. Regenerating

Run `luajit -joff tools/gen_quest_index.lua` (about 6 s for all five clients) **after any generator that rewrites a
route file** (`gen_wiki_guides.py`, `gen_gold.py`, `gen_professions.py`, `gen_completion_guides.py`). The bake does
not change route text and needs no re-run. `luajit -joff tools/gen_quest_index.lua --check` says whether the files
there are current; `tools/test_quest_counts.lua` fails on a stale or missing entry.
